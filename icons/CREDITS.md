## open-crop-icons (OpenFarm)

Most icons here come from [open-crop-icons](https://github.com/openfarmcc/open-crop-icons)
by OpenFarm and its contributors, taken at commit
`d41f1197a14bb82f12037dc47585152ae1e3074f` (November 2026). Its README says:
"These are released under a CC0 license. They're public domain works so use
them without restriction." It also asks for "a credit somewhere to help other
people find them", which this file is. Thank you to everyone who drew them.
